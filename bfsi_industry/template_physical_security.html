Dear Banking Operations, Security, Risk & Compliance Leaders,

SITUATION

Banking leaders oversee security governance across branches, ATMs, offices and cash-handling locations—often involving multiple teams, vendors and shifts.

The governance gap: policy versus branch practice

PAIN A documented control does not always show whether exceptions were logged, assigned, escalated and closed consistently. IMPACT Unclear ownership can delay investigations, fragment reporting and surface audit or compliance gaps.

TRIGGER EVENT

An upcoming branch review, audit, compliance assessment, vendor renewal or expansion is a practical moment to test whether records and execution align.

DECISION CRITERIA

Review exception ownership and closure by location; incident-report completeness and evidence; handover and escalation timeliness; and repeat findings or overdue actions. Compare results with your internal requirements rather than assuming every branch needs identical controls.

Owners for exceptions and follow-up

03 Readiness

Prioritized checks before the trigger
