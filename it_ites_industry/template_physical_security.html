Reviewing Site Coverage

Technology campuses coordinate employee, visitor, and vendor access across office and operational spaces.

Access routines that vary by building or shift can make oversight harder for workplace teams. Unclear post responsibilities or shift handovers can leave operations teams with gaps to investigate.

A useful impact measure is access exceptions and incident response time; reviewing your own baseline can help show where attention is needed without assuming a result.

If a site review, renewal, or planning cycle is approaching, it can be a practical time to revisit these controls.
